# How to use Panorama

## Filters
The filter bar at the top of each dashboard page sets the period, the region and country, the category, sub-category, and product, and more filters such as segment, status, shipping method, payment method, and discount level. The filters are in the page address, so a link shows the same view. Locked chips show the limits of your role.

## Gross and net revenue toggle
The Gross/Net toggle in the filter bar changes the revenue charts between gross revenue (all statuses) and net revenue (without cancelled and returned orders).

## Drill-down
A click on a bar for a region, a country, a category, or a product filters the page to that item. The breadcrumb under the filter bar shows the path, for example All › Europe › Germany, and a click on an earlier item goes back.

## Comparing periods
Select a period in the filter bar. The KPI cards then compare it with the same period one year earlier, with like-for-like dates. The Compare page compares any two periods or groups side by side.

## Explorer
The Explorer page builds a custom view: select up to six metrics, up to two dimensions, a time grain, and filters. It shows a chart and a pivot table. You can download the data as CSV, copy a link, save the view in your browser, and ask the assistant about the result.

## Orders and exports
The Orders page lists the orders of your scope with server-side paging. A click on an order opens its details. Fields that your role cannot see show "Hidden for your role". The CSV export has at most ten thousand rows and the audit log records it.

## Asking the assistant
The assistant answers questions about your data in plain English. It shows the result table first, then a short explanation with citations. Simple questions get an exact template answer at once. Compare, trend, and "why" questions get an AI explanation. When the AI is busy, you get the correct table at once with a short notice.

## Good questions for the assistant
Ask about a metric for a period and a group, for example revenue of a category in a quarter, the top countries of a region, the return rate by shipping method, the trend of orders by month, or why a number changed between two years.

## Data Notes page
The Data Notes page explains every metric, every column, the formulas, and the known data quality problems, with evidence from your own scope.
